# First KDP Book Roadmap Guide

A calm, end-to-end manual for publishing your first book on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) — without hype, without tricks, and without stress you didn't need.

**By Asdale Dynamics, LLC**

---

## Who this guide is for

- You have knowledge worth sharing, and a book seems like the right way to share it.
- You have never published on KDP before, and the dashboard looks intimidating.
- You want real, workable steps — not promises about making money while you sleep.
- You want to publish *one good book* and learn the process properly, rather than mass-produce junk.

This guide is written by the people behind *The Massive AI Manual* — a book that explains AI tools and concepts "like a friend would" — published through KDP, the slow and ordinary way, and shipped as a real product. Nothing here is theoretical for us. The lessons in this guide come from actually doing it.

## Who this guide is *not* for

- Anyone looking for traffic tricks, review schemes, or category-hacking that trades short-term numbers for reader trust.
- Anyone who wants legal, tax, or accounting advice. See the disclaimer below.

## The roadmap at a glance

1. **Pick the right first project.** One reader, one problem, plain English. (Chapter 1)
2. **Build the interior deliberately.** Page by page, with KDP's layout rules in mind. (Chapter 2)
3. **Price honestly.** Understand the royalty math before you set a number. (Chapter 3)
4. **Amplify, don't manipulate.** Categories and keywords describe your book truthfully. (Chapter 4)
5. **Launch quietly.** A calm launch week beats a chaotic one. (Chapter 5)
6. **Do the paperwork once, and do it right.** Royalties, records, and forms. (Chapter 6)

## How to use this guide

Read chapters 1 and 2 before you write a single page of your manuscript. Chapters 3 through 6 can be read in order, or consulted when you reach each stage. The [checklists](checklists.md) page condenses every chapter into a checklist you can follow while working.

## Important disclaimers

- **Education, not legal advice.** This guide explains concepts and processes in plain English. It is not legal advice, tax advice, or accounting advice. For decisions with legal or money consequences, talk to a qualified professional.
- **Details change.** KDP's rules, royalty percentages, printing costs, and page-count thresholds are shown here with the values we used and know from experience, but Amazon updates these numbers. Our experience: **always verify against KDP's current help documentation** before committing. We point to the exact help pages where rules you'll care about live.
- **No affiliate links, no upsells.** This guide earns nothing when you sign up for anything.
