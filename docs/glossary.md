# Glossary

Every acronym and term in this guide, in plain English. Terms are ordered alphabetically. The reference chapter for each is given.

## A

**ASIN** — *Amazon Standard Identification Number.* Amazon's own product identifier. Your ebook gets an ASIN automatically on KDP; it has no ISBN. (Ch. 6)

**Author copies** — Printed copies of your own paperback that KDP prints and sells to you at cost (printing charge only). Useful for proofs, signings, and review copies.

## B

**Bestseller Rank / BSR** — Amazon's continuously computed per-category popularity ranking. Widely surveilled; nearly meaningless for the first-book decision process. (Ch. 5)

**Bleed** — Extra printed area (0.125") that runs past the finished trim edge and is cut off, so photos/backgrounds can reach the edge with no white sliver. "No bleed" means your content stays inside margins, which is the simpler beginner path. (Ch. 2)

## C

**Categories** — Amazon's browse-classification slots (3 on KDP at the time of writing). Choose for accuracy, not for competition-mining. (Ch. 4)

**Copyright page** — The page of formal legal notice, usually verso (the back) of the title page. Year, owner, rights statement. (Ch. 2 and 6)

## D

**Delivery fee** — In the ebook 70% royalty tier, KDP deducts a per-megabyte fee (documented as $0.15/MB in the US at the time of writing). Lean text keeps it small; big image files make it grow. (Ch. 3)

**Disclaimer** — A short paragraph saying what the book doesn't claim — e.g., education-only, not professional advice. (Ch. 2)

## G

**Gutter** — The inside margin, kept larger than the outside margins because binding eats into it. It depends on page count; look yours up on KDP's spec page, don't guess. (Ch. 2)

## I

**ISBN** — *International Standard Book Number.* A 13-digit identifier for a specific edition/format of a book. Paperbacks use one; the ebook uses an ASIN instead. KDP offers a **free KDP-assigned ISBN** (recommended default for most first books) or you can supply your own. (Ch. 6)

**Interior** — The manuscript file (ebook or print) that contains everything inside the covers, uploaded to KDP.

## K

**KDP** — *Kindle Direct Publishing.* Amazon's self-publishing platform.

**KDP Select** — Amazon's optional program of promo tools (free days, etc.) available at the cost of 90-day ebook exclusivity to Amazon. (Ch. 3)

**Kindle Previewer** — Amazon's free desktop app for checking how your ebook renders; also the in-dashboard previewer. The phone-app check is your second opinion. (Ch. 2)

**Koll / Kindle Owners' Lending Library** — Legacy borrow programs on Amazon. Not relevant to a beginner decision — noted here just to say so.

## L

**List price** — The price you set that readers see. Your royalty is computed from list price minus fees (and minus printing cost for paperbacks). (Ch. 3)

**Look Inside** — Amazon's sample-preview feature on the product page. Correct rendering here is a launch-day check item. (Ch. 5)

## M

**Manuscript** — The book's content file. Interior file in KDP's accepted formats. Not to be confused with "the book you're shopping to a traditional publisher" (different meaning elsewhere).

## P

**Page break** — A hard instruction that pushes following content to the next page. Chapters in an ebook start with one. (Ch. 2)

**Proof copy** — Author copy of your own paperback ordered before publishing. Reading it is the highest-value quality step in this guide. (Ch. 2 and 5)

## R

**Royalty rate** — Your per-sale percentage/tier: 35% or 70% for ebooks (with price thresholds), a fixed ~60% of list minus printing for paperbacks. Values in this guide are historical; **recompute against KDP's current rates**. (Ch. 3)

## S

**Styles (Word/Google Docs)** — Named formatting presets (Heading 1/2/3, Body) that KDP converters understand and that generate TOCs correctly. The opposite of manual formatting hacks. (Ch. 2)

## T

**Trim size** — The finished physical size of a printed book, e.g., 6"×9". Your manuscript page setup must match it exactly. (Ch. 2)

**TOC** — *Table of contents.* Clickable links in ebooks; page numbers in print. (Ch. 2)

## W

**W-9** — US tax form you or your US entity fills out for KDP so payment can happen; name/ID must match IRS records. (Ch. 6)

**W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E** — For non-US individuals/entities: forms establishing foreign status and treaty benefits for any royalty withholding. KDP's tax interview generates the right one. (Ch. 6)

**Withholding tax** — Tax deducted at source on royalty payments, mainly relevant for non-US authors and treaty-dependent. Confirm your situation with a professional. (Ch. 6)

## Numbers that change

Royalty percentages, delivery fee, printing-cost formula details, margin/gutter thresholds, and keyword/category slots are **KDP-documented values that Amazon updates**. This guide's works are grounded in real experience publishing *The Massive AI Manual* — the method is durable; the numbers must be re-verified per book.
